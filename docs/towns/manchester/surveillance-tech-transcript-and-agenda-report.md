## Licence-plate searches by Manchester police

Six Connecticut towns have released the Flock audit log of every search run against their cameras. Below is what that record holds for Manchester's own police department -- not a finding, just the log.


**Manchester CT PD**

- **61,300 searches** between 2023-01 and 2026-07 (43 months with at least one search)
- **215 camera networks** swept by the average single search
- **93%** of them reached beyond Manchester's own cameras
- The commonest stated reason is free text or a numeric code, rather than one of the preset categories Flock introduced in December 2025

**[Open Manchester CT PD's search dashboard](../../analysis/towns/manchester-alpr-searches.html)** -- every one of those searches, filterable by month, stated reason, search type and how far it reached, with a **[How to read this](../../analysis/towns/manchester-alpr-searches.html#how)** tab covering what each field means and what the record cannot tell you.


> **A floor, not a total.** The record only sees queries that reached those six towns' cameras. A single Flock query typically sweeps thousands of networks at once, so most of a Connecticut department's searching does land in it -- but the real count is higher. Nothing in it names an officer, shows a plate, or records what any search returned.
