# Cameras implemented with no legal process

Towns where surveillance cameras (ALPR/Flock, drones, or similar) went into
use without a town ordinance, a legally noticed public hearing, or a
recorded vote authorizing them — contrasted, where possible, against how
the same town handles far more mundane matters.

- **[East Lyme Surveillance-Tech Transcript & Agenda Report — East Lyme](../towns/east-lyme/surveillance-tech-transcript-and-agenda-report.md)** (2026-08-23) — Full meeting-transcript and agenda/minutes scan (2012–2026) for East Lyme, CT. No ordinance, public hearing, or recorded vote authorizing Flock/ALPR cameras has ever been found on record — despite a named Selectman formally documenting the cameras and recommending a surveillance-technology inventory in writing as early as January 2025.
