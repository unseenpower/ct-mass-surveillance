# Departments with search records in our database

**56 Connecticut police departments, 446,146 licence-plate searches**
between January 2023 and August 2026, from six towns' released Flock audit logs.

Each card is a summary. Click one for that department's full dashboard — filter
by month, stated reason, search type and how far each query reached, and read
every reason its officers typed, with personal names, licence plates, case
numbers and street addresses masked. Each carries its own **How to read
this** tab.

!!! warning "These are floors, not totals"

    A department appears here only for searches that reached the cameras of the
    six Connecticut towns whose audit exports have been released. Its real
    search count is higher, and a department absent from this list has not been
    shown to search less — it means no released log happens to record it.

    For example, as of September 24, 2026, West Hartford has not released its
    own audit log. So its page shows only the West Hartford searches that also
    reached a camera in one of the six towns that have (Darien, Farmington,
    Manchester, North Haven, Waterford and Windsor). A typical West Hartford
    query sweeps hundreds of camera networks at once, so most of its searches
    do reach one of the six: checked against USA TODAY's separately published
    Flock search data, these logs held every West Hartford search in each of
    the 24 months both sources cover. What cannot appear here is a search of
    West Hartford's own cameras alone, or another department searching West
    Hartford's cameras.

    **"Reached beyond its own cameras"** is the share of a department's searches
    that swept more than one camera network. A query sweeping thousands is a
    nationwide search, not a local one.
