# Analysis

Longer interactive pieces built from the audit data. These are standalone pages —
they open outside the docs navigation because they carry their own layout and
graphics, and they are meant to be readable and shareable on their own.

Unlike the per-town reports, everything here is a **claim**, not a raw keyword
match. Each page states its sources and its caveats at the bottom.

## [The Task Force Exception](the-task-force-exception.html)

**Fusion centers, RISS and Connecticut's ALPR network.** Five Connecticut towns'
Flock audit logs record 12,894,062 searches against their cameras between January
2023 and June 2026. Only 3.4% were run by a Connecticut agency, and 59.3% swept
more than 5,000 camera networks at once.

Among the rest are all four regional RISS centers, nine fusion centers and nineteen
federal agencies. NESPIN — the New England node, and a Massachusetts charity rather
than a police department — sent **43.8% of every Flock search it has ever run
nationally** into one of those five towns.

Public Act 26-14 fences this in from 1 October 2026, restricting sharing to
Connecticut, New York, Rhode Island and Massachusetts agencies — plus "a
multijurisdictional task force of which a public agency or law enforcement agency
of this state is a participating member." **The act never defines that term**, and
it is the only category NESPIN fits.

The page includes an interactive force-directed map of every intelligence entity
and federal agency that reached those five town networks, filterable by category.
